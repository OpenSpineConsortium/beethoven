# Your chat window cannot open your data. Your own Claude Code worker on the cluster can.

You already ask an AI about your thesis, your first paper, the grant text, the code, mostly on a free chat tier. It is useful until the real work starts.

## What a free chat cannot do

A chat window sees what you paste into it. It cannot open the folder your data lives in, run your script against the real dataset, or check that the figure in your draft matches your table. When the chat ends, your project is gone from it.

## What changes when the worker lives on the cluster

Beethoven gives you your own Claude Code worker, running as a job on your university's cluster, in your project folder. It reads your code and your data where they are, runs the analysis there, edits the files and reruns the pipeline. Ask why a figure changed and it looks in your files and shows you what it found. You stay the author; it does the digging.

## Work that can be checked

Reviewers ask for the code. Search committees look for it. Work done in your project folder stays there, where your advisor or a reviewer can check it: the scripts that made the tables are the scripts in the repository, not a conversation you cannot find again. The difference is a worker that touches the real data, not a chat that describes it.

## From your laptop or your phone

You reach the worker inside claude.ai, on desktop or phone, and start, watch and stop it from the Beethoven extension, through the Open OnDemand portal session you already have.

## Your sign-in, your lab's list

You sign in to your university and to Claude yourself. Beethoven never stores your password and never touches your Claude login. Your lab lead approves who gets access: one approved account, one installation. Once approved, you get Beethoven from a private Chrome Web Store listing.

## What it costs

Your own Claude Pro subscription, about $20 a month. Set that against the pasting, re-explaining and copying back by hand.

**Ask your lab lead, or request access at https://openspineconsortium.com/beethoven/**

Beethoven runs Claude Code and is not an Anthropic product; "Claude" and "Claude Code" are Anthropic's marks, used here to say what it runs; "Open OnDemand" is the mark of its publisher.
